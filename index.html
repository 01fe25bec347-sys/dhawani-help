    How Dhawani works

    A simple, transparent path from your words to a resolved ticket.

    Step 01

    Report

    Type, speak or add a photo in Hindi or English.

    Step 02

    Understand & confirm

    Dhawani detects the language, issue, place and urgency, then asks you to confirm.

    Step 03

    Route

    The ticket goes to the concerned department and appears on the officials dashboard.

    Step 04

    Track resolution

    Follow status updates with your ticket ID until the issue is resolved.

    How it is built

    Pipeline

    Citizen channel → language detection and speech-to-text → LLM extraction → confirmation → ticket database → department router → dashboard.

    Roadmap

    Claude API extraction, Bhashini speech and translation, WhatsApp and Telegram bots, a real vision model, SMS and missed-call access, CPGRAMS integration.

    Privacy

    Minimal data: the complaint, an approximate place and a ticket ID. No names or phone numbers are needed. Low-confidence cases are confirmed with the citizen.

    Change your complaint area

    New complaints will be routed to the local authority for this area.
